1. Macro Industry Patterns & Strategic Synthesis

Auth Dynamics

OAuth2 dominates user-delegated SaaS (65%), essential for CRM, Helpdesk, and Project Management.

  • API Key & Bearer Tokens (40-46%): Dominant in Developer Infra and Data Scraping for machine-to-machine speed.
  • Hybrid Auth (18%): Modern leaders (GitHub, Supabase, Linear) support both OAuth2 and granular Personal Access Tokens.

Gating Friction

77% of apps provide friction-free self-serve sandboxes or free tiers for instant credential generation.

  • The 23% Gated Barrier: Concentrated in Enterprise CRM (DealCloud), Finance (PitchBook), and Ads (Google/LinkedIn Partner Review).
  • Marketing Trial False Flag: Marketing "Free Trial" buttons often mask enterprise sales gates for APIs.

MCP & Quick Wins

75% of apps have active MCP servers or native toolkits (21 Official MCPs, 49 Community, 5 Composio native).

  • 83 Immediate Quick Wins: Scoring 8-10 with open REST/GraphQL specs and instant token generation.
  • 10 High-Touch Outreach Apps: Require partner contracts or commercial vetting (Paygent, DealCloud).
